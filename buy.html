Buying

Onslow County buyer's problem isn't inventory.

It is information. Half the houses you will compete for never sit on the market long enough for a public search to matter. Representation is what closes that gap, and it is the entire reason to have an agent at all.

How We Work

Five steps. No surprises.

You will know the fee, the timeline and the strategy before we tour a single house.

The honest intake

Budget, timeline, non negotiables, and the things you say you want but will trade away by week three. We write it down.

Market calibration

Real numbers on the specific streets you care about. What is selling, what is sitting, and why. This is where most expectations get fixed.

Targeted touring

Six to twelve houses, not thirty. Including the ones not publicly listed yet, because we ask the listing agents before the sign goes up.

Offer architecture

Price is one of nine terms. We build the offer around what this particular seller actually needs, then defend the appraisal and the due diligence window.

Contract to keys

Inspection, repair negotiation, appraisal, lender check ins twice a week, and a final walkthrough that is not a formality.

New Construction

The builder's agent works for the builder.

They are pleasant, they are helpful, and they are legally on the other side of the table. Bring your own representation to the first visit, because most builders will not let you add an agent after you have registered on your own.

  • Contract review before you sign, including the arbitration and change order language.
  • Incentive negotiation on rate buydowns, closing costs and design center credits.
  • Third party inspections at framing, pre drywall and final. Not the builder's inspector.
  • Punch list enforcement after closing, when the builder's attention has moved on.
