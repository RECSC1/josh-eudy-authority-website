PCSing to Camp Lejeune: Should You Buy or Rent? The Math Most Guides Skip

By Josh Eudy·October 7, 2026·Military Relocation

Most buy-or-rent advice for Lejeune stops at "it depends." It does depend, on things you can actually check from wherever you are stationed now. Here they are, including the insurance math that changed this year.

The short answer

Rent if your orders are under two years or could change. Buy if you will be here two to three years or longer and the house would rent well after you leave. Use your VA loan, compare your BAH to the full monthly payment including insurance, and get real homeowners and flood insurance quotes before due diligence ends, since coastal Onslow County rates rose about 32 percent over 2025 and 2026.

I served in the Army, so I know what a set of orders does to a timeline. Families PCSing to Camp Lejeune or MCAS New River ask me the same first question almost every time: should we buy or rent? Here is how I answer it, honestly, including the cases where I tell people to rent.

1. How long will you actually be at Lejeune?

  • Under two years, or orders that could change: renting usually wins. Buying and selling both cost money, and a short stay rarely gives the house enough time to cover that.
  • Two to three years or more: buying starts to make sense, especially if you would keep the house and rent it out when you leave.
  • Not sure you will like the area: rent for a year first. A lease in the wrong neighborhood costs far less than a mortgage in the wrong neighborhood.

2. Ask whether the house would rent after you leave

This is the question that decides most Lejeune purchases. Onslow County has a steady stream of military renters, so the right house can become a rental when your next orders come. The wrong house, even a nice one, can sit. Before you buy, ask what it would rent for, how fast similar houses lease, and what a property manager would cost if you are stationed somewhere else. I buy and own property here myself, so I will give you the landlord math, not just the buyer math.

3. Does a VA loan make buying at Lejeune worth it?

  • Eligible borrowers can often buy with no down payment.
  • There is usually a VA funding fee, and veterans receiving VA disability compensation are generally exempt. Ask your lender to confirm yours.
  • The benefit can be reused, and in some cases you can keep one VA loan on a rental and still buy at your next duty station. Your lender can tell you how much entitlement you have left.

4. Run your BAH against the full payment, not the price

Look up your current BAH for your rank and dependency status on the Defense Travel Management Office calculator. Then compare it to the full monthly cost of the house: principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, flood insurance if needed, and any HOA. That full number is the one that has to fit, and on the coast, insurance is the part that surprises people.

5. How much did homeowners insurance go up in Onslow County?

North Carolina approved a two-step homeowners insurance increase. The second step took effect June 1, 2026. For the coastal territories that include Onslow County, the combined increase over 2025 and 2026 is about 32 percent, and no new base rate filings can take effect before June 1, 2027. If you are comparing a house in Jacksonville to one closer to the water, get real quotes on both before your due diligence period ends.

6. Check the flood zone before you fall in love

Ask for the flood zone and any elevation certificate on every house you are serious about. Flood insurance can change the monthly payment as much as the price does, and maps get revised. Jacksonville has been updating its flood maps, so confirm the current zone with the City or your insurance agent rather than an old listing.

7. When renting is the smarter move, I will say so

If your orders are short, the numbers do not work, or the house would not rent well later, renting is the right call, and I can help you find the right rental too. My job is to get you into the right place for your orders, not to sell you a house.

Send me your report date, your gate and your BAH, and I will run both scenarios for you, buy and rent, side by side. You can also compare the towns around base or see how I represent buyers.

This is general information, not lending or insurance advice. Confirm your VA eligibility and funding fee with your lender and your insurance costs with a licensed agent.
