How It Works

Two numbers. You pick.

Most "we buy houses" sites are one company trying to buy as cheap as possible. I am a licensed broker and a real buyer, so you get both sides of the math before you decide anything.

01  Tell me about it

Address, condition and what is going on. Takes two minutes. Nothing is signed and nothing is listed.

02  Get both numbers

My cash offer to buy it directly, as is, and my honest estimate of what it would sell for if we listed it, minus what it would take to get there.

03  Pick the one that fits

Take the cash and close on your timeline, list it with me, or do nothing. No pressure either way, and no hard feelings if the answer is no.
