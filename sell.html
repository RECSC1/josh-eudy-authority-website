Selling

Your house gets one week of real attention.

Everything we do is built around that week. Pricing, prep, media, timing, and the buyers we call before the sign goes in the yard. What happens in the first seven days sets the price you end up with, whether you sell in nine days or ninety.

Home Valuation

Find out what your home is actually worth.

Not the automated estimate. A real number, from a person who has walked the comparable houses on your street and knows which ones had a new roof and which ones took water in Florence.

You will get a range, the three comps that drive it, and an honest read on what one week of prep would do to the top of that range. No obligation, and we will tell you if this is a bad time to sell.

You will get a real range within one business day, not an automated estimate.

The Listing Plan

What your listing actually gets.

Not a bullet list on a proposal. This is the work, and it happens on every listing regardless of price.

01  Pricing strategy

Block level comps, three modeled price points, and a written recommendation with the reasoning attached. You make the call with the same information we have.

02  A punch list with real numbers

What to fix, what to leave alone, and what each item is worth at the closing table. Most sellers spend money on the wrong things. You will not.

03  Full media in one visit

Professional listing media, coordinated and scheduled so your listing goes live looking like it should.

04  Pre market outreach

The buyer agents working your price band get a call before the sign goes up. This is where the first offers usually come from.

05  Search and social distribution

Syndication, a real property page that ranks, paid placement in the right zip codes, and a Google Business Profile post the day it goes live.

06  Day ten and day twenty one review

Showing data, feedback, and a decision. If something is not working we say so out loud and change it.
