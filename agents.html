How I Work

Real volume. Still my phone.

Ninety three closings in a year is not a hobby. It is a system. But the system is built so that you still deal with me, not a junior agent and not a call center.

  • One point of contact. Me, start to finish. The strategy, the showings and the negotiation do not get handed off.
  • A transaction coordinator on every file. Dates, documents and deadlines get tracked by someone whose whole job is that, so nothing slips between contract and closing.
  • Two numbers when you sell. A listing plan and, when it makes sense, a cash offer from me directly. Side by side.
  • I refer out. If your search is outside what I know well, I will say so and hand you to someone better.
