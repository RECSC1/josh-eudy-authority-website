Tool 01

Neighborhood Matcher

Six questions. Onslow County is not one market, and the difference between Hubert and New Bern is not a preference, it is a completely different set of tradeoffs. This narrows twelve towns down to the three worth your Saturday.

How This Works

Answer on the left. Your matches show up here.

We weight budget and commute hardest, because those two eliminate more towns than everything else combined. School assignment is deliberately not a question, because in Onslow County it is decided address by address and it moves. That one we check together.

  • Twelve towns scored across budget, commute, priorities, housing age and how you spend a Saturday.
  • Top three returned with a fit percentage and the honest tradeoff for each.
  • No email required. Take the results and go drive them yourself if you want.

      One Warning

      A calculator has never won a house.

      These are good for narrowing. They are useless for deciding. Every one of these numbers moves when you put a real address into it, and the ones that move most are the ones that cost the most: school assignment, flood zone, HOA reserves, the roof, and what the seller actually needs.
