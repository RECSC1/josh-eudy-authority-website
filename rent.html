Renting

Renting is a strategy, not a consolation prize.

A twelve month lease in the right area buys you the one thing no buyer can purchase later: time to be sure. We help renters land the right place, and we help owners price and place the right tenant.

For Renters

Get the house, not the runner up.

Good rentals in Onslow County go in days, and the deciding factor is almost never the applicant with the highest income. It is the one whose paperwork was ready. We tell you exactly what to have on hand and we submit for you the day it lists.

  • Pre screened inventory including owner listings that never hit the public sites.
  • Same day applications with your documents already packaged.
  • Lease review so you know what you are agreeing to on pets, repairs and early termination.
  • Relocation leases with virtual tours for families arriving from out of state.
Start a rental search

For Owners

Income without the phone calls.

Whether you moved and kept the house or bought it on purpose, the job is the same: the right tenant, the right rent, and a maintenance process that does not fall on you at midnight.

  • Rent benchmarking against actual signed leases, not asking prices.
  • Tenant screening on credit, income, rental history and eviction record.
  • Full management? Ask. I will tell you exactly what I handle and who I trust for the rest.
  • Placement only if you would rather manage it yourself.
Talk to us about management
