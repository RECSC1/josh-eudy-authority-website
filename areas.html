The Read On Each

What we would tell a friend.

Jacksonville, NC

The core market. Camp Lejeune and MCAS New River drive the calendar, inventory turns on orders rather than on seasons, and the buyer pool refreshes every summer.

Sneads Ferry, NC

Onslow County, on NC-172 and NC-210. A working fishing town on the New River, minutes from the Stone Bay and Courthouse Bay areas of Lejeune and the bridge to North Topsail. Waterfront lots, newer neighborhoods and real boat access.

Surf City, NC

The heart of Topsail Island, on both sides of the Onslow and Pender line. Full time residents, second homes and short term rentals, all priced by distance to the sand. Flood zone and insurance come first here.

Hubert, NC

Onslow County, on NC-24 and NC-172 between Jacksonville and Swansboro. Quiet, close to the back side of base, and closer to the water than the price suggests.

Richlands, NC

North Onslow County, off NC-24 and US-258. Small town feel, land and acreage, and room to spread out.

Swansboro, NC

Onslow County, on NC-24 at the White Oak River. A historic waterfront downtown, newer neighborhoods inland, and Emerald Isle a short drive away across the Cape Carteret bridge.

Holly Ridge, NC

Onslow County, on US-17 minutes from the Surf City bridge, without the island price. New neighborhoods going up steadily.

Hampstead, NC

Pender County, on 17 between Jacksonville and Wilmington. Soundside neighborhoods, golf communities and households that split a commute between the two.

Maysville, NC

A small Jones County town on US-17, at the edge of the Croatan National Forest. Land, quiet, and lower entry prices than the coast.

Beulaville, NC

Duplin County. Acreage, agriculture and buyers who want distance on purpose.

New Bern, NC

Craven County, about forty five minutes up US-17. A historic downtown where the Neuse and Trent meet, close to Cherry Point, and real waterfront at inland prices.

Topsail Beach, NC

Pender County. The quiet south end of Topsail Island, mostly second homes and rentals.

Area Questions

Onslow County, without the brochure voice.

Inland almost always beats coastal on square footage. Richlands, Maysville and Beulaville give you the most land for the money. Holly Ridge gives you newer construction close to the beach without island pricing. The right answer depends on which gate you report to, so start there.

Assignment matters more than town. Onslow County uses a base assignment plan with magnet and choice options, and boundaries move. Never buy a house on a school assumption without confirming the current assignment for that specific address.

It depends on the gate and the hour, not the mileage. Sneads Ferry to Stone Bay is short. Surf City or Hampstead to Main Gate at morning report is a real drive on 17 or 210, and the bridge adds its own math. That is exactly why we are building a gate by gate drive time table at real report times instead of quoting map distance.

Inventory has loosened at the top and stayed tighter in the price bands most military buyers shop, which is why the story sounds different depending on who you ask. The countywide median hides that, so we price by band and by street, not by headline.
