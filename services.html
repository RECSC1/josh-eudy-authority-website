What Each One Means

No mystery line items.

New Construction & Builder Representation

The builder's agent works for the builder. We read the contract, negotiate the incentives, and hold the walkthrough to a standard.

Military Relocation & PCS Moves

Video tours, drive times to your gate at real report times, BAH math by rank and town, flood zone and insurance checks, and a closing timeline built backward from your report date. From an Army veteran and Military Relocation Professional.

Home Prep, Staging & Renovation

A punch list, a budget, and vetted trades.

Photography, Video & Listing Media

Professional photos and video tours on every listing, shot by a local photographer, so your house shows up online looking the way it does in person.

Mortgage & Lending Partners

Rate locks, bridge options and a pre approval that listing agents respect.

Closing Attorney & Title

North Carolina closes with attorneys. Title work starts the day you go under contract, a transaction coordinator tracks every deadline, and there are no surprises at the closing table.

Leasing & Rental Placement

Rent benchmarking, tenant placement, and an honest answer on whether to sell or rent when the orders come. If you need full management, ask, and I will tell you exactly what I handle and who I trust for the rest.

Investors, Flips & 1031

Cash flow math on real Onslow County numbers from someone who buys and flips here himself. Rentals, flips, lots and land, exchange timelines, and an honest answer on whether the deal is worth doing.

Inherited, Land & Problem Properties

An estate to settle, a lot you are not going to build on, a house that needs more work than you want to do, or a payment you are behind on. You can list it, or I can make you a cash offer directly. Either way you get honest math and a straight answer, fast.

Visibility

Your house gets found or it doesn't.

A listing that only lives on the MLS is relying on other agents to do your marketing. We treat every listing like a small campaign: a property page that ranks, syndication that actually completes, a Google Business Profile post the day it goes live, and paid placement pointed at the zip codes your buyer is sitting in.

  • Property pages built to be searched, with the address, neighborhood and schools written the way people type them.
  • Google Business Profile updated on every listing, price change and close.
  • Social and email to a list of local agents and past clients, not a generic blast.
  • Reporting you can read, showing where the traffic came from and what it did.
