New Construction & Builder Representation

The agent sitting in the builder's model home is paid by the builder. They are pleasant, they are helpful, and they do not represent you. Bring your own broker to the first visit, because most builders will not let you add one after you have registered.

What that representation is actually worth: reading the builder contract, which is written for them and is not the standard North Carolina form you would see on a resale; negotiating incentives, which usually move further on closing costs and upgrades than on base price; holding the pre-drywall and final walkthroughs to a real punch list; and telling you honestly when a lot premium is not worth paying.

Josh works closely with builder reps across Onslow and Pender, including the new communities going up around Holly Ridge, Richlands and the 17 corridor. Ask which builders are currently offering what, because it changes quarter to quarter.

Military Relocation & PCS Moves

Josh is an Army veteran and a Military Relocation Professional, and most of this work happens with a client who has never stood in the house.

What that looks like in practice: video walkthroughs on your schedule and your time zone; drive times measured to your actual gate at your actual report time, not straight-line distance on a map, which matters enormously when the TOP Gate is closed on weekends and the Piney Green Gate runs inbound only until 1700; BAH math by rank and town so you know what your allowance really covers here; flood zone and insurance checked before you fall in love with something; and a closing timeline built backward from your report date.

If orders come early and you need to decide between selling and renting it out, you get both answers from one person, because Josh also buys and holds property here himself.

Home Prep, Staging & Renovation

Most sellers spend money on the wrong things. They repaint a room nobody mentioned and ignore the thing every buyer will walk out over.

So this starts with a walkthrough and a written punch list in priority order, with a real number next to each item and an honest note on which ones you should skip. Carpet, paint, pressure washing, landscaping and lighting usually return more than a kitchen does at this price point in Onslow County. A full renovation almost never pays for itself on a 90 day timeline.

From there: vetted local trades who will actually show up, scheduling that fits your listing date rather than fighting it, and staging where it earns its cost. If the right answer is to list it as is and price for the work, Josh will tell you that instead of selling you a project.

Photography, Video & Listing Media

Your listing gets about one week of real attention. The photos decide whether anyone shows up for it.

Every listing gets professional photography from a local photographer who shoots this market, plus video where the property justifies it. Not a phone walkthrough, and not the agent's own camera roll. Where it helps, that extends to drone, twilight exterior and floor plans.

The part most agents skip is what happens after the shoot. The media has to land everywhere at once: the MLS, the syndication feeds, a property page that is actually written to be searched, a Google Business Profile post the day it goes live, and social that reaches local agents and past clients rather than a generic blast. Great photos sitting only on the MLS are a wasted invoice.

Mortgage & Lending Partners

Josh is not a lender and does not take a cut from one. What he has is a short list of local loan officers who answer the phone on a Sunday and who listing agents in this county take seriously.

Why that matters here specifically: a large share of this market runs on VA loans, and a VA offer written by someone who does not understand the appraisal and minimum property requirements gets beaten by a weaker conventional offer all the time. The fix is a lender who can speak to the listing agent directly and a pre approval that reads as real.

Also covered: rate locks and float downs, bridge and recast options when you are buying before you sell, and a straight answer on whether waiting for a better rate actually saves you anything on the house you want.

Closing Attorney & Title

North Carolina is an attorney closing state. You do not use a title company the way you did in Texas or Florida, and that surprises most buyers moving in.

What that means for you: an attorney handles the title search, the title insurance commitment, the deed and the disbursement. Title work starts the day you go under contract, not the week of closing, because a boundary problem, an old lien or an unreleased mortgage takes time to clear and finding it late is what blows up closing dates.

Also worth knowing before you write an offer here: North Carolina uses a Due Diligence Fee paid directly to the seller and a Due Diligence Period during which you can walk for any reason. That fee is generally not refundable. Understand both numbers before you sign, because out of state buyers routinely do not.

Leasing & Rental Placement

Orders come early, a house will not sell at the number you need, or you bought here on purpose to hold it. All three end up in the same conversation.

That conversation starts with honest rent benchmarking against what is actually leasing in that neighborhood right now, not what is listed. Then the real math: rent against mortgage, taxes, the current insurance number, maintenance and vacancy. In this market the insurance line is what breaks the spreadsheet, particularly anywhere coastal.

If the numbers work, Josh handles tenant placement, screening and the lease. If you need full ongoing property management he will tell you plainly that it is not what he does, and point you to who he trusts. If the numbers do not work, he will tell you to sell and show you why.

Investors, Flips & 1031

Josh buys, flips and holds in this county himself, with a business partner, including land, lots and a mobile home park. You are getting numbers from someone with his own money in the same market, not someone quoting a podcast.

What that covers: cash flow analysis on real Onslow County rents and real current insurance quotes; rehab budgets based on what trades actually charge here this year; land and lot deals, including the ones that need a perc test before they are worth anything; and manufactured and mobile home transactions, which most agents in this market quietly avoid.

On 1031 exchanges, the clock is the whole game. You have 45 days from closing to identify replacement property in writing and 180 days to close. Line up your qualified intermediary before you sell, not after. Josh will tell you honestly when a deal is not worth doing.

Inherited, Land & Problem Properties

An estate to settle, a lot you are never going to build on, a house that needs more work than you want to take on, or payments you are behind on. These are the situations most agents quietly decline, and they are a core part of what Josh does.

You get two numbers instead of one. A listing plan with a realistic price range and what it would take to get there, and a direct cash offer from Josh buying for his own account. Then you pick, with both numbers in front of you. Most sellers in this position have only ever been shown one.

Practical notes: an inherited property usually cannot be sold until the estate has authority to sell, so the first call is often to the clerk of court or an attorney rather than to a Realtor. Heirs property with multiple owners takes longer and needs everyone aligned early.

Visibility

Your house gets found or it doesn't.

A listing that only lives on the MLS is relying on other agents to do your marketing. We treat every listing like a small campaign: a property page that ranks, syndication that actually completes, a Google Business Profile post the day it goes live, and paid placement pointed at the zip codes your buyer is sitting in.

  • Property pages built to be searched, with the address, neighborhood and schools written the way people type them.
  • Google Business Profile updated on every listing, price change and close.
  • Social and email to a list of local agents and past clients, not a generic blast.
  • Reporting you can read, showing where the traffic came from and what it did.
