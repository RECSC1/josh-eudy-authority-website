Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026.

This site is operated by Josh Eudy, a licensed North Carolina real estate broker (#300792) with Realty ONE Group Revolution. This page explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

When you fill out a form, we receive what you type: usually your name, email, phone number, property address and your message. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank details or payment information on this site, and you should never send them through a form.

Like most websites, the site and its host may also record basic technical information such as your browser type, the pages you visited and the approximate location of your internet connection. We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand which pages are useful.

How we use it

Form submissions go directly to Josh so he can answer your question, prepare a price range, make or discuss an offer, or follow up about the move you asked about. We do not sell your information. We do not share it with lead buyers or portals.

If your request requires it, your information may be shared with people involved in your transaction, such as a lender, closing attorney, inspector or the other party's agent, and only for that purpose.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, Josh or his team may call or text you about your request. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of further texts. Message and data rates may apply.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records as long as needed to serve you and to meet North Carolina real estate recordkeeping requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us at any time. Email homessoldbyjosh@gmail.com or call (910) 333-5433.

Service providers

This site is hosted on Netlify, which processes form submissions on our behalf. Links to other websites, such as Zillow, Google or government sites, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
