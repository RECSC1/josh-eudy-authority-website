Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity

Last updated October 7, 2026.

Josh Eudy and Realty ONE Group Revolution are committed to the letter and spirit of the federal Fair Housing Act and the North Carolina State Fair Housing Act.

We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability or familial status, or any other class protected by federal, state or local law, in the sale, rental, financing or advertising of housing.

How that shows up in our work

  • We describe properties and areas, not the people who live there.
  • We do not rank neighborhoods or towns by school quality or demographics. For school assignment, we point you to the school district's address lookup for the specific property.
  • We show every client every home that fits their criteria, wherever it is.
